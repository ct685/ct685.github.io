01 — Personal Project

Transpose

From Pinterest board to Depop search

The problem

A Pinterest board captures a vibe, but not a shopping list. Transpose turns a board of outfit images into concrete, shoppable searches on Depop — “sheer halter satin top” instead of just “y2k.”

How it works

  • Embeds each board image with FashionCLIP and averages them into one “vibe vector”
  • Ranks ~44k labeled fashion items by cosine similarity to find the closest pieces
  • Tags the board’s aesthetic and garment details with zero-shot classification
  • Predicts the occasion with a trained classifier on frozen embeddings
  • Builds Depop search links from aesthetic + detail + garment

Stack

Python · PyTorch · FashionCLIP · Streamlit

Stride Across the Pacific

The case
